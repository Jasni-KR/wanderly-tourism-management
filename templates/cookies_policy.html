What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device to help improve your browsing experience. They allow websites to remember your preferences and activities.

How We Use Cookies

  • To improve site performance and speed.
  • To analyze website traffic and user behavior.
  • To remember your preferences for future visits.

Managing Cookies

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. However, please note that some features of the website may not work properly if cookies are disabled.
